Open the homepage
What to tryTrip page

On the trip page

  • Scroll down to the days. The map plays the whole trip on a loop, then draws each day's part as you reach that day. Scroll back up and the line goes back with you.
  • Before you reach the days, press pause on the map, then play.
  • Change the hotel level on the price card. The price follows there, in the enquiry form and on the bar at the bottom.
  • Watch the bar at the bottom. It arrives after the first screen and steps aside while the price card or the form is on screen.
  • Go through both steps of the enquiry. It checks the name and the number, and sends nothing.
What to tryHomepage

On the homepage

  • Under the place of the video, a sentence fills with ink, word by word, as it crosses the screen.
  • "Kashmir, slowly." shrinks to a title and four photographs take the middle in turn, while the page holds still.
  • At the winter trips, keep scrolling: the row runs to its last card before the page moves on. You can also swipe the row yourself.
  • On a laptop a small label follows the pointer over a trip. Drag the row with the mouse and it says Drag.
  • Open the first trip. The other cards have no page here.
At the topTwo switches

The switches in the dark strip

  • Motion switches every effect off, so you can see the same pages standing still. A visitor whose phone asks for less motion sees them this way from the start.
  • Smooth scrolling shows on laptops only. It makes the mouse wheel glide, and it starts switched off so you can compare.
  • On a keyboard, M and S press the two switches.
  • A phone held sideways is too low for the held scenes, so there the pages stand still.
Where it mattersReal phones

Where to try it

  • Safari on an iPhone.
  • Inside Instagram: send yourself this address in a message and open it there.
  • Inside Facebook, the same way.
  • An older Android phone.
  • An iPhone with Low Power Mode on.
Stand-insNot final

What is not real here

  • The homepage video is a plain block. The real one stays exactly as it is.
  • "Kashmir, slowly.", the sentence that fills with ink and the four season photographs are stand-ins for you to choose.
  • The photographs on the trip cards and at the top of the trip page are stand-ins from the free-to-use part of your Instagram folder.
  • Enquire, WhatsApp, the menu and the enquiry form do nothing.
  • Only the Kashmir Snow Weekend has a page.
